Schedule

Every evening, on one.

The week on VBN 1 from first light to sign-off. Times are Xareionople time, and the line-up for tonight is always the one on the front of this page.

    VBN 1 carries the VNC Evening Bulletin at 19:00 every day by agreement with the VNC Trust. The bulletin is made and edited by VNC, not by VBN; its full listings are on the VNC site, and the day’s stories on the VNC Newsroom. Entertainment, ratings and charts are on VBN Entertainment. Schedules may change for live sport and for state occasions.

    News & entertainment

    The news is not ours.

    VBN 1 is an entertainment channel that carries the country’s public news. The bulletin you see at 19:00 is VNC’s, made under a statute that keeps this building out of its newsroom. We give it the hour and the picture, and we never touch it.

    VNCHeld in trust

    VNC is the Federation’s public news service, created in 164 AAX and held in the statutory VNC Trust. Its headlines, bulletins and regional services live on its own site.

    Go to VNCRead the Newsroom →

    VBN carries VNC at its own cost and gives it the 19:00 hour. We do not edit it.

    Where to watch

    Wherever the signal goes.

    VBN 1 is on Channel 1 across Latrevia, and reaches the neighbouring realms through our own stations and partners.

    Latrevia

    Channel 1

    From the Compound in Xareionople, carried by our transmitters at Lancia Hill and Ezechiel and by cable across the states.

    Solaria

    VBN Solaria

    Our regional station for the Kingdom, carrying VBN 1’s evenings with French-language programming of its own.

    Paradeisos

    VBN Paradeisos

    The island’s own station, with VBN 1’s evenings and local programmes from the harbour.

    Luisara

    LPN

    The Luisara People’s Network, a private VBN subsidiary under Luisaran law, carries selected VBN 1 programmes in Spanish, alongside its own regional programming.

    Nicolatia

    NPBC

    Selected VBN programmes air on the Nicolates Public Broadcasting Channel under a carriage agreement. NPBC chooses its own titles.

    Regnum

    Not on air

    Regnum is a separate planet, reached by portal and not by transmitter. We ran VBN Colline and VRS Triumterra there until Triumterra seized both in 138–141 AAX. Nothing airs there under VBN today.
