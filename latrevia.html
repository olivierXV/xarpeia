Five branches

How the Federation is governed

Power is split across an executive, a legislature, courts, and two independent commissions that answer to no elected officeholder.

Head of state

Zarpaii Xarpeius, President

Elected in 164 AAX in the first presidential election under the Third Republic. The President is also President of the Commonwealth by virtue of the office, and sits at the Executive Palace in Xareionople. Two terms of five years is the limit, whether consecutive or not. She is also Queen of Solaria, a personal exception the Commonwealth Charter allows for this officeholder alone (Art. 31).

Executive · Art. 10–12

President and Cabinet

  • President elected directly, five-year term, commander-in-chief.
  • Prime Minister leads the Cabinet and can be removed only by a constructive vote of no confidence.
  • Ministers confirmed by the House.
Legislative · Art. 13–17

Federal Assembly

  • Senate two senators from each of the 26 states, six-year staggered terms.
  • House proportional representation, at least three members per state, four-year terms.
  • Sectoral seats twenty percent of the House.
  • Visit the Federal Assembly website
Judicial · Art. 18–20

Courts

  • Supreme Court a Chief Justice and eight Associate Justices.
  • Constitutional Court seven judges on non-renewable twelve-year terms.
  • Federal judges serve to age 75.
  • Visit the Federal Courts website
Examination · Art. 21–23

Civil Service Commission

  • Seven commissioners, staggered ten-year terms.
  • Runs public examinations and publishes results.
  • Appoints federal judges on merit and enforces the affirmative action programmes.
Audit · Art. 24–26

Control Commission

  • Five commissioners, eight-year terms.
  • Audits public spending and investigates any federal official.
  • Starts impeachment proceedings before the House.

Also set up by law: the Federal Electoral Commission, whose members the Civil Service Commission appoints; an independent media regulator; the Federal Police Service for federal offences; and an Intelligence Review Judge drawn from the federal bench. Intelligence services answer to the Prime Minister and to a standing oversight committee of the Assembly.

How a bill becomes law

  1. Introduced by any member of either chamber, or by the Cabinet.
  2. Passed by a simple majority in both chambers. Budget bills start in the House.
  3. The President has fifteen days to sign or return it with objections. Silence while the Assembly sits makes it law.
  4. A veto is overridden by two thirds of both chambers.
  5. Challenge before the Constitutional Court; a law it strikes down is void from the date of ruling.
Schedule A

The twenty-six states

States keep their own constitutions, governments and courts. They run local government, schools, primary and secondary healthcare, policing under federal minimum standards, and land use (Art. 27).

Federal territory · Art. 3, 48

Mt. Xarpeius Protected Federal Territory

A forest reserve east of Erimos around Mt. Xarpeius and the Cassius Flower Forest. It is a permanent heritage reserve open to all citizens, and it cannot be privatised, given to any state, or opened to extractive industry. No emergency power can suspend its protections.

Constitution of the Third Republic

Adopted 1 Ixarius, 162 AAX

Written in answer to the Theocracy of Iliyos and the Imperial Republic. Every institution above is a response to one of the two.

IustitiaJustice

The law shields the weak and punishes the guilty. It is never a weapon of the powerful.

LibertasLiberty

No citizen is silenced, chained, or compelled to worship or to serve.

AequitasEquity

Fairness is measured by need, not by sameness.

Never again a theocrat. Never again an emperor. Never again a crown without consent.

Andra

A member of the predominant sapient species of Latrevia. Human is an acceptable colloquial synonym.

Eulon

Any sapient being of non-Andra origin recognised as a person. Eulons hold the same civil, political and social rights as Andra citizens.

Omega

A protected sex category distinct from male and female, with identical legal status. No law may treat omega status as a disqualification.

Title XI · Art. 49–52

Part of the Commonwealth of Latrevia and Solaria

The Federation is a founding constituent entity of the Commonwealth, a single sovereign state of two equal entities. Defence, foreign policy, the Latrum currency, free movement and Commonwealth citizenship are handled jointly. Everything else stays with the Federation and its states.
